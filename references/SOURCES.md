## Primary-source verification

Use current official regulatory text for legal conclusions; use DDTC/BIS guidance for program design and primary provider documentation for exact service capabilities. Record the accessed page and date in each engagement. The following are starting locations, not a claim that a page was checked during skill preparation:

- ITAR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/chapter-I/subchapter-M
- EAR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-VII/subchapter-C
- NISPOM: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/subtitle-A/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-117
- Rule changes and effective dates: https://www.federalregister.gov/
- DDTC: https://www.pmddtc.state.gov/
- BIS: https://www.bis.gov/
- User-supplied DDTC guidelines link: https://deccs.pmddtc.state.gov/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=cc037c571bdd7150c6c3866ae54bcbc6

Do not substitute NISPOM definitions for ITAR definitions without verifying applicability. Preserve exact agency, title, part, section, and edition. S10 is 15 CFR Part 730; a prior reference to Part 170 was clarified in the inventory.
